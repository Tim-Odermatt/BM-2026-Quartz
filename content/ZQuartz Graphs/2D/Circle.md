# Circle

Circle creates a circle at the given x,y point and then takes a radius `[[x,y], radius]`.

````yaml
```graph
bounds: [-10, 10, 10, -10]
keepAspectRatio: true
elements: [
    {type: circle, def: [[0,0], 3]}
]
```
````
