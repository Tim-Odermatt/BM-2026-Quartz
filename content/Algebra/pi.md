Ratio from a circles circumference to the diameter.
$$
\begin{matrix} d = \mathrm{diameter}
\\C = \mathrm{circumference}
\\
\\{\displaystyle \pi ={\frac {C}{d}}} \approx 3.14159...
\end{matrix}
$$
# Modern day calculation
A computer (or better a server) running the [y-cruncher](https://www.numberworld.org/y-cruncher/) programm set to [Chudonsky algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chudnovsky_algorithm) from 1988, while it is rather weird calling it just a computer when the main limitation just a gigantic and very fast storage space is.

Newest record set on 2025.11.18 by StorageReview with [314'000'000'000'000](https://www.storagereview.com/review/314-trillion-pi) digits of pi after a total runtime of 110 days.
