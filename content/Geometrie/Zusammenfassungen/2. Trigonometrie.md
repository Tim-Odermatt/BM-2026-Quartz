$$
\sin(\alpha) = \frac{a}{c}= \frac{GK}{H}\\
\cos(\alpha) = \frac{b}{c}= \frac{AK}{H}\\
\tan(\alpha) = \frac{b}{a}= \frac{GK}{AK}
$$
## [Arkusfunktion](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkusfunktion)
Die Inversfunktion von den sin, cos und tan.
$$
\alpha = \arcsin\left(\frac{a}{c}\right)= \arcsin\left(\frac{GK}{H}\right)\\
\alpha = \arccos\left(\frac{b}{c}\right)= \arccos\left(\frac{AK}{H}\right)\\
\alpha = \arctan \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)= \arctan \left(\frac{GK}{AK}\right)
$$

## Genaue sin, cos und tan Wert

|               | 0°  | 30°                    | 45°                    | 60°                    | 90° |
| ------------- | --- | ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------- | --- |
| $\sin \alpha$ | $0$ | $\frac{1}{2}$          | $\frac{\sqrt{ 2 }}{2}$ | $\frac{\sqrt{ 3 }}{2}$ | $1$ |
| $\cos \alpha$ | $1$ | $\frac{\sqrt{ 3 }}{2}$ | $\frac{\sqrt{ 2 }}{2}$ | $\frac{1}{2}$          | $0$ |
| $\tan \alpha$ | $0$ | $\frac{\sqrt{ 3 }}{3}$ | $1$                    | $\sqrt{ 3 }$           | -   |
