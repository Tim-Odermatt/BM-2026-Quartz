# The Great Atomic Grammatizator
## Center of the story and what it does?
A machine capable of producing a immitation of any type of text, by copying a larg list of text and kind of remixing them into something that feels new, but is just a copycat in discuise.
## Main Characters and their needs and wants from the machine?
### Mr. Bohlen the buisnessman
He is not really the inventer nor is he the one capable of writing texts, but upon he sees all the money and fame he gains from it, he is fully with mr. Kneipes ambitions. Later he turns into a fameous writer.

### Mr. Knipe the Engineer
The brains of the operation. He created the first calculator and later the machine. He writes short stories in his free time, but is not succesfull with it. Later after he created the machine and had to teach mr. Bohl how to use it, they both turn into fameous writers.
## Benefits and risks of this machine?
