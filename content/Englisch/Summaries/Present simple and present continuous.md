# Present simple
A verb usually with a -s end.
- Every day he ==goes== home. (a REGULAR event)
- Some days he ==goes== home. (a FACT or EVENT that is usally true)
- Warm air ==holds== more water vapour. (a Scientific FACT)
- I ==need== to go home (a state verbe usually does not have a continuous form)
- If I ==go== home now, then I can not ==enjoy== the sunset. (If clause)
- Now ==take== of your glasses. (a INSTRUCTION, DEMONSTRATION or EXPLAINATION)
- The book ==starts== with a murder, then they ==eat== food. (A NARRATIVE summory or REPORT)
- Flames ==surround== the entire forest in LA. (newspaper/media HEADLINES)
## State verbs
1. State meaning normally favor Present simple
know, underrstand, believe, doubt, need, want, prefer, belong, contain, seem, appear, matter, own
2. stative or dynamic
I ==think== the policy is inadequate. (my OPPINION)
They are ==thinking== about changing the policy. (a TEMPORARY event)
3. "Be" can describe a characteristic or temporary behaviour.
I am ==being== overly cautious. (a TEMPORARY behaviour)
I am overly cautious. (a FACT about me)
# Present continuous
A verb with a -ing end and a carry verbe to set the mood.
- I am ==going== home. (a NOW event)
- I am always ==walking== fast. (a REPEATED BEHAVIOR)
- Temporarly I am not ==working==. (a TEMPORARY pattern/event/behaviour)
- I can see the sun ==shining==. (a COMMENT on the event NOW)
